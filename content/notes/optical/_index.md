These are rough notes from learning about computer architecture and asking how optical hardware might change it. They are exploratory, sometimes incoherent, and likely wrong in places. Treat claims, numbers, and descriptions of research projects as questions to verify, not established facts.

Start with [Computer architecture](/notes/optical/computer-architecture/) and [Comp arch and microarch](/notes/optical/comp-arch-and-microarch/) for the computing questions. Then read [Optical computing](/notes/optical/optical-computing/) and [Optical architecture](/notes/optical/optical-architecture/) for the main thread. [Akehtonics arch paper](/notes/optical/akehtonics-arch-paper/) records reactions to one proposed architecture; [Fourier optics](/notes/optical/fourier-optics/), [Optical image processing](/notes/optical/optical-image-processing/), and [Non-linear optics](/notes/optical/non-linear-optics/) are background notes.

The remaining notes cover digital building blocks, logic, and possible projects. They are fragments rather than a course or a coherent argument.
